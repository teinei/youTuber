Based on **image.png**, the plant is **Lambsquarters** (*Chenopodium album*), also commonly known as goosefoot or fat-hen.
While it is a highly nutritious plant that wild quails frequently use for coverage and forage, here is what you need to know about feeding it to your birds:
 * **The Stems:** You can technically give them the stems, but quails generally won't eat them. Lambsquarters stems become quite fibrous, tough, and woody as the plant grows.
 * **The Leaves and Seeds:** These are the parts your quails will actually love. Quails will happily peck at the tender green leaves and the tiny black seeds.
 * **A Quick Caveat (Oxalates):** Like spinach, lambsquarters contains oxalates. In moderation, it is an excellent, vitamin-rich treat, but you shouldn't let it completely replace their primary feed.
**Recommendation:** Instead of stripping the stems, you can cut a leafy branch and hang it or drop it directly into their enclosure. They will strip off the leaves and seeds themselves and leave the tough stem behind.
