To clarify a quick point about how music metadata works: **albums themselves do not have an ISRC.** Instead, an **ISRC** (International Standard Recording Code) belongs strictly to individual *tracks* (recordings). The album as a whole is assigned a **UPC** (Universal Product Code) or EAN.
If you are distributing an album through the distributor **SpaceMedia**, you can easily retrieve the individual ISRCs for all the tracks on that album using the methods below.
### Method 1: Check Your SpaceMedia Dashboard
Since SpaceMedia automatically assigns ISRCs for free when you upload your music (or allows you to input your own), the most direct place to find them is right inside your catalog.
 1. Log in to your **SpaceMedia.uk** account.
 2. Go to your **Catalog** or **Releases** tab.
 3. Click on the specific album or EP you want to view.
 4. Look at the tracklist metadata section. Each song listed will have its 12-character alphanumeric ISRC displayed right next to or underneath the track title.
### Method 2: Check the Delivery Confirmation Email
When SpaceMedia successfully reviews and approves your multi-track release for distribution to streaming platforms (like Spotify and Apple Music), they typically send a confirmation email. This summary often includes the final metadata sheet containing:
 * The Album UPC code.
 * The individual ISRC codes for every track on the release.
### Method 3: Use an Online ISRC Finder (If Already Live)
If the album has already been delivered to streaming platforms and is live on Spotify, you don't even need to log into SpaceMedia. You can use a public lookup tool:
 1. Copy the **Spotify share link** for one of the tracks from your album.
 2. Go to a free lookup tool like the **Soundplate ISRC Finder** or **Soundcharts ISRC Finder**.
 3. Paste the link to instantly extract that specific track's 12-character digital fingerprint. (Repeat for the other tracks on the album as needed).
### Method 4: Use the Official IFPI Database
If your tracks have been live for a little while, they will be indexed in the official global registry.
 1. Go to the **IFPI International ISRC Search Tool**.
 2. Search by your **Artist Name** and the **Track Title**.
 3. The database will return the exact assigned codes for those recordings.
