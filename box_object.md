The small box with "OBJ" inside that you are seeing is called an **Object Replacement Character**.
### What is it?
It is not an error caused by a virus or anything wrong with your phone or computer. It is a **placeholder symbol** used in digital text when your device encounters an object, emoji, or special character that it cannot display or recognize.
### Why does it appear?
This usually happens because of a mismatch between different systems:
 * **Unsupported Emojis:** Someone may have sent an emoji from a newer version of an operating system (like a new iOS update) to a device running an older version that doesn't have that specific emoji in its library.
 * **Software Incompatibility:** You might be viewing content in an app or browser that hasn't been updated to support certain modern characters or formatting.
 * **Data Corruption:** Sometimes, during the process of copying and pasting text from one place to another (like from a website into a document), formatting or embedded media data gets "lost" or corrupted in translation. Your device sees that *something* was supposed to be there, but it can no longer render it, so it shows the "OBJ" box as a signpost instead.
 * **Voice-to-Text Glitches:** Occasionally, speech-to-text tools create "objects" in the background that don't translate correctly into plain text, leaving these placeholders behind.
### Is it a problem?
No, it is **harmless**. It is simply the system’s way of saying, *"I know there is something here, but I don't know how to show it to you."*
If you see these often, updating your apps or your device’s operating system to the latest version usually solves the issue, as it will likely add the missing character support you need.
