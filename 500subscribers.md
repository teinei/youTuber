**At 500 subscribers, you can apply for the expanded YouTube Partner Program (YPP) for early access to some monetization features, but not full ad revenue yet.** Full ads (AdSense payouts from video ads) still require 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 valid public watch hours (last 12 months) **or** 10 million valid public Shorts views (last 90 days).

### What You Get at 500 Subs (Expanded YPP Tier)
If you also meet these:
- 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days
- 3,000 valid public watch hours (last 12 months) **or** 3 million valid public Shorts views (last 90 days)

You can join YPP early and unlock:
- Fan funding (Super Thanks, channel memberships, Super Chat/Stickers)
- Shopping features
- Some other tools

**Full ad revenue unlocks at the 1,000 sub tier.** Many creators apply at 500 for the extra features and then push toward full monetization.

### Should You Open an AdSense Account at 500 Subs?
**Yes, but only create it through YouTube Studio when you apply to YPP.** Do **not** create one separately on the AdSense website early — it can cause issues (duplicates get rejected). YouTube will guide you to set up an "AdSense for YouTube" account during the application process.

### What to Do When You Hit 500 Subs
1. **Check eligibility in YouTube Studio** → Go to the **Earn** tab.
2. **Apply to YPP** when you meet the thresholds.
3. **Set up AdSense** as prompted in Studio (only one account per payee name).
4. **Go through review** (can take ~1 month or longer).
5. Once accepted, enable the features you want (fan funding, etc.).

### How to Prepare Now (Before or at 500 Subs)
- **Focus on consistent, high-quality original content** — Avoid reused, repetitive, or low-effort videos (YouTube reviews your whole channel).
- **Build watch time** aggressively (playlists, strong hooks, end screens, cards).
- **Upload regularly** (at least 3 public videos in any 90-day window).
- **Follow all policies**:
  - Community Guidelines (no strikes)
  - Monetization policies
  - Advertiser-friendly content guidelines
- **Enable 2-Step Verification** on your Google Account.
- **Optimize for growth**:
  - Good thumbnails + titles
  - SEO (keywords in titles, descriptions, tags)
  - Engage with viewers (replies, community posts)
  - Post Shorts if that fits your style (they help with views/subs fast)
- **Analytics check** — See what’s working and double down.
- **Tax/Payment info** — Have your address, bank details, and tax info ready (W-8BEN if outside US, etc.) for when payments start.

**Pro tip**: Even before 500, aim for the 1,000 sub / 4k hours goal. Many people hit 500 and then slow down — keep the momentum.

Once you're in YPP (even early), keep uploading actively. Inactive channels (no uploads for 6+ months) can lose features.
