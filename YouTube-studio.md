**YouTube Studio** is the main hub for managing your channel. "Wrapping up" videos typically means adding **End Screens** (also called end cards) — interactive elements that appear in the last 5–20 seconds of your video to direct viewers to subscribe, watch more content, playlists, or links.

These help "wrap up" the video experience, boost engagement, watch time, and session views. You can also use **Cards** (interactive pop-ups during the video) for similar purposes. Beyond that, Studio's **Analytics** and **Editor** tools help you improve video performance.

### How to Add End Screens in YouTube Studio

1. Sign in to [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/).
2. From the left menu, select **Content**.
3. Click the title or thumbnail of the video you want to edit.
4. In the left menu, select **Editor**.
5. Click **End screen**.
6. Choose an element to add:
   - **Video** — Promote a specific video, your latest upload, or best for viewer.
   - **Playlist** — Link to a public playlist.
   - **Subscribe** — Button to subscribe to your channel.
   - **Channel** — Promote another channel.
   - **Link** — External website (requires YouTube Partner Program).
   - Or **Apply template** for pre-grouped elements you can customize.

You can add up to 4 elements (for standard 16:9 videos). Drag to adjust timing (they appear in the last 5–20 seconds), position them on screen, preview, and **Save**.

**Tips**:
- Your video must be at least 25 seconds long.
- End screens are not available for "Made for Kids" videos.
- You can also add/customize them during the initial upload process under **Video elements**.

### Cards vs. End Screens
- **Cards**: Interactive elements (e.g., video links, polls, links) that pop up at specific timestamps you set during the video. Go to the video details > **Cards** on the right panel.
- **End Screens**: Larger, more prominent elements reserved for the end of the video. Use both for maximum engagement.

### Improving Your Videos with YouTube Studio

**1. Use the Built-in Editor** (for post-upload tweaks):
- In the **Editor** tab: Trim/cut sections, blur areas, add audio from YouTube's library, adjust end screens/cards, and add ad breaks (if monetized).

**2. Leverage Analytics to Optimize**:
- Go to **Analytics** in the left menu.
- Key metrics to watch:
  - **Audience Retention** → See where viewers drop off and fix pacing, intros, or boring parts.
  - **Impressions & CTR** (Click-Through Rate) → Improve titles/thumbnails.
  - **Watch Time** → The algorithm loves this; use end screens/playlists to keep viewers on your channel.
  - **End Screen** reports → Check click rates on specific elements and iterate (e.g., test different videos or CTAs).
- Use **Advanced Mode** for deeper reports, comparisons, and exports.

**3. Best Practices for Better Performance**:
- Keep end screens clean (2–3 elements max) and time them with your outro.
- Direct viewers intentionally (e.g., "Watch this next" playlist) instead of letting the algorithm choose.
- Test what works: Check which end screen elements get the highest clicks and replicate them.
- Combine with strong calls-to-action in your video script.
- Monitor traffic sources, demographics, and top-performing videos to inform future content.

**4. Other Improvement Tools in Studio**:
- **Content** tab: Bulk edit metadata, visibility, etc.
- **Monetization** and **Copyright** checks.
- Dashboard for quick performance overviews.
- Respond to comments and check notifications for engagement.

End screens and cards are quick wins that can significantly increase views, subscribers, and session watch time. Start by adding them to your recent videos, then use Analytics to refine your approach over time. For the latest interface details, always check directly in YouTube Studio, as features evolve.
